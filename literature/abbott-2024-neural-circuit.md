# Neural Circuit Diagrams: Robust Diagrams for the Communication, Implementation, and Analysis of Deep Learning Architectures

[arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.05424) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2402.05424)
## Relevancy to the research
Highly relevant to the question of how a useful category of architectural syntax could be constructed. It could be interesting to study the effects on different priors on these diagrammatic representations. It could also act as a great bridge where the abstract developing theory meets implementation.

This paper (its introduction mostly) also acts as a great position statement about what a scientific theory of deep learning actually would consists of:
- Theoretical insight should be intertwined with practical insight, like new architecture designs or more efficient algorithms.
- Concepts and claims should be reproducible without specific, exact reference to accompanying code. 
The introduction is inspiring, and I would love to write a shorter note on what I believe a scientific theory of deep learning ought to do for us.
