# Weaves, Wires, and Morphisms: Formalizing and Implementing the Algebra of Deep Learning

[arXiv abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07242) · [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.07242)

## General summary
Develops a more systematic mathematical foundation for the language, with the ambition of eventually integrating the optimization techniques of the second paper.

## Relevancy to the research
Uses Neural Circuit Diagrams, para construction, and array-broadcasted categories to build a categorical framework for array operations and broadcasting, with executable implementations.

Abbott's work provides a plausible route from mathematical descriptions of architectures to mathematical manipulations of architectures. A framework of priors could provide criteria for which of those manipulations are structurally meaningful.
