# physical-axioms

## Question
What *physical axioms* of deep learning exist? A theory in physics is not bounded by the mathematics it uses, but by the physical assumptions it abstracts. For example, calling general relativity a "Lorentzian gravity" misses the point. The bedrock of general relativity is based on a set of physical assumptions obtained from observations, usually stated purely in words: "The local effects of gravity are equivalent to the effects of uniform acceleration", "The laws of physics must take the same mathematical form in all coordinate systems", etc. What are the analogous such statements in deep learning?
