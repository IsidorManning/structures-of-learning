# structure-of-priors

## Question
Do priors require symmetric monoidal structure, cartesian structure, multi-sorted cartesian structure, or a more general doctrine depending on the kind of prior? Partly why logical priors and geometric priors give rise to the same notion of a "prior" in Nye's work is that finitary monads on $\mathbf{Set}$ are equivalent to Lawvere theories. What about non-finitary priors? Are there any examples of such priors?
