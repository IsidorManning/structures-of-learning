## Question
Empirically, is there a relationship between respecting a prior and generalizing on the related task? For example, for any model that generalizes on a task, is there an underlying, well-defined prior that the model respects? We could ask the contrapositive of this as well. If I have two identical models, trained identically, put one is instantiated from a prior, will there be a statistically significant difference in their generalization on the relevant task?
