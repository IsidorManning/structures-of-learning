## Questions
In neuroalgebraic geometry, one studies the space of parametric models, called *neuromanifolds*, defined as:
$$\mathcal{M}:=\{ f_p : X \to Y \mid p\in P\} = \phi_P(\mathcal{C}(X,Y)),$$
where $\phi_P$ is the parameterization mapping $f\mapsto f_p$.

One of the open questions is about understanding how structures in data can be reflected by geometric features of the neuromanifold represents a more general and fundamental question [Section 6, [[../../../literature/marchetti-2025-algebra-unveils|An Invitation to Neuroalgebraic Geometry]]]. In the context of priors, this asks about the nature of the geometric relations between priors and neuromanifolds, which indeed is a broad yet exciting question that perhaps could benefit from a categorical perspective?
